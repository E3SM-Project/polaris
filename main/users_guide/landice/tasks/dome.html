dome

The dome tasks evolve an ice dome on a flat bed, the test Compass called dome. Each task runs with MALI’s shallow-ice velocity solver (sia) and with Albany’s first-order solver (fo):

landice/dome/2km/sia/smoke_test
landice/dome/2km/sia/decomp
landice/dome/2km/sia/restart
landice/dome/2km/fo/smoke_test
landice/dome/2km/fo/decomp
landice/dome/2km/fo/restart

The tasks share the landice/dome/2km/init step, which makes the mesh and initial condition. The first-order tasks need a Polaris environment with Albany (see Albany).

mesh

The mesh is a planar hexagonal mesh with 2-km resolution and 30 by 34 cells, nonperiodic in both directions, with 10 uniformly spaced vertical levels.

initial conditions

The ice thickness is a Halfar dome with a radius of \(r_0 = 60\sqrt{0.125}\) km and a maximum thickness of \(h_0 = 2000\sqrt{0.125}\) m:

\[h(r) = h_0 \left[1 - \left(\frac{r}{r_0}\right)^{4/3}\right]^{3/7}\]

for \(r < r_0\), and zero elsewhere. The dome is centered on the cell nearest the center of the domain, which is moved to the origin. The bed is flat at sea level.

forcing

The surface mass balance is zero.

time step and run duration

The time step is 1 year. smoke_test runs for 200 years with the shallow-ice solver and for 2 years with the first-order solver. decomp runs for 2 years, and restart compares a 2-year run with a 1-year run that restarts from its first year.

config options

# config options for dome tasks
[dome]

# sizes (in cells) for the uniform mesh
nx = 30
ny = 34

# number of vertical levels in the mesh
levels = 10

# the dome type ('halfar' or 'cism')
dome_type = halfar

# Whether to center the dome in the center of the cell that is closest to the
# center of the domain
put_origin_on_a_cell = True

# whether to add a small shelf to the test
shelf = False


# config options related to visualization for dome tasks
[dome_viz]

# the colormap for ice thickness
thickness_cmap = cmo.ice_r

# the colormap for surface speed
speed_cmap = cmo.speed

A cism dome has a parabolic profile, \(h_0\sqrt{1 - (r/r_0)^2}\). With shelf = True, the bed drops to 2,000 m below sea level for \(x < -10\) km.

cores

The model runs on 4 cores, except in decomp, which compares runs on 1 and 4 cores.

smoke_test

smoke_test runs MALI and plots the ice thickness and surface speed at the end of the run in its viz step.

decomp

decomp runs MALI on 1 and 4 cores and compares the thickness and normal velocity. With the shallow-ice solver, they must be bit-for-bit. With the first-order solver, they must agree within the norms that Compass used.

restart

restart runs MALI for 2 years in full_run, and for 1 year in restart_run, which starts from the restart file full_run writes after its first year. The thickness and normal velocity at the end of the two runs must be bit-for-bit with the shallow-ice solver. With the first-order solver, results differ slightly between runs placed on different cores, so they must agree within the same norms as in decomp.
