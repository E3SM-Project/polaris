dome

The dome tasks in polaris.tasks.landice.dome evolve an ice dome on a flat bed with MALI’s shallow-ice and first-order velocity solvers (see dome). add_dome_tasks() adds a smoke_test, decomp and restart task for each solver.

framework

The tasks share a config file, dome.cfg, and the steps below.

init

The class polaris.tasks.landice.dome.init.Init is a step shared by all the tasks. It makes a planar hexagonal mesh, culls and converts it, turns it into a MALI mesh with polaris.landice.mesh.make_mali_mesh() and adds the dome’s thickness, a flat bed and zero surface mass balance.

forward

The class polaris.tasks.landice.dome.forward.Forward is a polaris.landice.model.MaliModelStep that runs MALI on the dome mesh with a given velocity solver, from namelist.landice and streams.landice in the package. For the first-order solver, it uses the package’s albany_input.yaml.

validate

The class polaris.tasks.landice.dome.validate.Validate compares the thickness and normal velocity in the output of two forward steps, within given norms.

viz

The class polaris.tasks.landice.dome.viz.Viz plots the ice thickness and surface speed at the end of a run.

smoke_test

The class polaris.tasks.landice.dome.smoke_test.SmokeTest runs MALI on 4 cores, for 200 years with the shallow-ice solver and 2 years with the first-order solver, and plots the results.

decomp

The class polaris.tasks.landice.dome.decomp.Decomp runs MALI on 1 and 4 cores and validates that the results are bit-for-bit with the shallow-ice solver, and agree within Compass’s norms with the first-order solver.

restart

The class polaris.tasks.landice.dome.restart.Restart runs MALI for 2 years in full_run, and for 1 year in restart_run from the restart file full_run writes after its first year. Both runs write output only at the end of year 2, using streams.restart in the package, and the outputs must be bit-for-bit with the shallow-ice solver. With the first-order solver, Albany’s velocity depends slightly on how MPI tasks are placed on cores (by about 1e-21 m/s), and the two runs are usually placed differently, so the validate step uses the norms of decomp instead.
