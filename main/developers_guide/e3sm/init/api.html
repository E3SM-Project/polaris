Check that the cull masks describe a consistent set of domains.

Check that no land-locked cells survive in either ocean domain.

consistency.check_critical_passages(...[, ...])

Check that no cell on a critical ocean passage was culled from the ocean.

consistency.check_sea_ice_removed_regions(...)

Check that removing the cells in which sea ice would be trapped did not remove a whole sea from the ocean.

dc_edge_diagnostics.check_ocean_dc_edge(...)

Check that dcEdge in the ocean/sea-ice domain stays within bounds relative to the local ocean background cell width.

dc_edge_diagnostics.check_ocean_dv_edge(...)

Check that the shortest dvEdge in the sea-ice domain, the ocean without ice-shelf cavities, is not too short for the sea-ice time step.

land_locked.find_dead_end_cells(ds_mesh, ...)

Find the dead ends of a candidate ocean domain: the cells that the C-grid criterion removes, either because they have fewer than two active edges or because removing their neighbors leaves them so.

land_locked.remove_land_locked_cells(...[, ...])

Refine the ocean and ocean-without-cavities domains together, removing the cells in which the ocean cannot circulate and those in which sea ice would be trapped.

land_locked.remove_ocean_land_locked_cells(...)

Refine the ocean domain alone, removing the cells through which a C-grid cannot circulate and those that are not connected to the open ocean.
